### Own family

J1 (Claude Haiku 5.5) and J2 (GPT-6.2 Sol) gave the same score on 89 of 100 answers.

| | J1 minus J2, mean score |
|---|---|
| On the 24 Anthropic answers (J1's own family) | -0.04 |
| On the 76 other answers | -0.01 |
| **Own-family effect** (first minus second) | **-0.03** (95% interval -0.23 to +0.23) |

A positive effect would mean J1 favours its own family. J2 judges Anthropic answers in
the results, so J1's own family is never judged by J1 there; this check is about
whether that routing was needed.
