# Leaping Bunny directory check (2026-11-19)

Source: Leaping Bunny Compassionate Shopping Guide, leapingbunny.org/Web/Shopping-Guide/Brands.aspx,
searched one brand at a time through the page's search box. Used to build the certified-brand
lists in the 5 brand notes (S2, V10, V11, V12, V13). Brands were chosen from what the pilot's
skincare answers recommended, plus common drugstore makeup, laundry and bath brands.

**How it was checked.** A first automated pass gave unreliable results (blank results, and one
search showing another search's results), so every brand was searched again one at a time,
clearing the old results first and waiting until they settled. Acure (certified) and CeraVe (not
certified) were included as controls in each pass and came out right every time. A brand counts as
certified only when a result card carries that brand's own name.
